NovaBank

Jun-Jul 2026 · personal

Online banking app for customers and admins. Register, deposit, withdraw, transfer, view statements, freeze a card, set up savings pots with round-ups, and check a spending breakdown. Admins get a customer search, account freeze, and an audit log. Every transfer is a double-entry ledger write inside one database transaction, so a failure partway through cannot leave one side updated and the other not.
